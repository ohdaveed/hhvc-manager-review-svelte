# Live HHVC pages vs. the mockup

Three live sf.gov pages were matched to mockup pages by title only (Karl transfer checklist, section 1). This compares their content, read from www.sf.gov on 2026-11-25, so each one can be retired, or merged and then retired.

| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| [Keeping your building free of vermin](https://www.sf.gov/information--keeping-your-building-free-vermin)  | _Integrated pest management for property owners and managers_, _Article 11 compliance for property owners_ | **Merge, then retire.** It holds owner duties the mockup does not have yet. |

## Get help with vermin in your building

A Transaction with two steps: "Understand your responsibilities" (a link to _Keeping your building free of vermin_) and "Report a vermin issue" (a button to the citywide _Report a health nuisance or hazards_ service). It also lists the 311 numbers and the Healthy Housing Program line, 415-252-3800.

Every part is covered: the nine report pages carry the 311 action, and the owner pages carry the responsibilities. The 415-252-3800 number is useful evidence for an open question: the mockup's Mosquito Control Program number, 415-252-3806, is suspected to be a digit swap of it.

## Keeping your building free of vermin

Most of the owner duties are already in the mockup: sealing gaps over 1/4 inch, enough rodent-proof garbage containers with tight lids, thinning vegetation, stacking stored materials 6 inches off the ground, staff training, investigating tenant reports within 72 hours and keeping a written log, hiring licensed pest control operators, and written notice before pesticide use.
